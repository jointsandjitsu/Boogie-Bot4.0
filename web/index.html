What do these numbers mean?

Claude bills by the token — roughly 3–4 characters each. Every time you send a message, the entire conversation context is sent as input.

Input tokens
Everything sent to the model: your messages, the system prompt, tool results, and the full conversation history. This is usually the largest cost driver.
Output tokens
Everything the model writes back: text, code, and tool-call JSON. Output costs 3–5× more per token than input on most models.
Cache-write tokens
The first time a large static prefix (system prompt, context) is processed, Anthropic stores it on their servers. You pay a small write fee. Subsequent turns re-use it cheaply.
Cache-read tokens
When the cache hits, you pay ≈10× less than the normal input rate for those tokens. High cache-read counts are good — it means you're saving money.
Estimated cost
Computed from the token counts above using the rates in pricing.json. Switch to your actual plan in the Settings tab to see the API-equivalent dollar value of your usage.
